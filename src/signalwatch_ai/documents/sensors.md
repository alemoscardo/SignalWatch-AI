# Synthetic sensors

## Sampling and units
Fictional document, revision 1. Temperature in °C and motor speed in rpm are sampled every minute. Timestamps are UTC. The demo dataset covers 10 September 2026, 08:00 to 11:00 UTC.

## Missing data and limitations
Cooling circuit measurements, load and calibration data are unavailable. Do not present interpolated missing samples as observations. An interval without samples must be reported as missing data, not interpreted as normal operation.
