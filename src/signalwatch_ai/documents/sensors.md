# Synthetic sensors

## Sampling and units
Fictional document, revision 2. Temperature in °C and motor speed in rpm are sampled every minute. Timestamps are UTC. The continuous demo history spans 10 September 2026, 08:00 UTC to 11 September 2026, 08:00 UTC. The dataset metadata supplied with an investigation gives the exact available time range; older test datasets may be shorter.

## Missing data and limitations
Cooling circuit measurements, load and calibration data are unavailable. Do not present interpolated missing samples as observations. An interval without samples must be reported as missing data, not interpreted as normal operation.
