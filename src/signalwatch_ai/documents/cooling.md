# M-01 cooling guide

## Cooling evidence
Fictional demonstration document. Restricted coolant flow or a warmer coolant inlet can reduce heat removal. Assess inlet temperature and flow together. These measurements are not present in the demo; high motor temperature alone cannot confirm cooling failure.

## Suggested observation
Fictional demonstration document. Compare a temperature excursion with motor speed and available coolant measurements. Request missing measurements from a qualified operator; the agent cannot control a pump or valve.
