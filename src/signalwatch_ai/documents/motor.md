# Synthetic motor M-01

## Operating conditions
Fictional document, revision 1. The demo motor's nominal temperature range is 50 to 80 °C. The detector raises an alert when temperature is strictly above 80 °C. Simulated operating speed ranges from 0 to 1800 rpm. These are not specifications for real equipment.

## Temperature and speed
An increase in motor speed may accompany a temperature rise. Temporal correlation does not establish a cause: the dataset contains no mechanical load or electrical power measurements.
